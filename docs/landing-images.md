# Visual Beranda Konsulin Tech

Gambar dibuat dengan tool imagegen bawaan. Visual merupakan ilustrasi, bukan foto staf/klien sebenarnya atau screenshot produk. Layout, warna template, dan logo pengguna dipertahankan. Gambar asli template tetap tersedia.

## Prompt set

### Hero — hero.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portrait hero photograph for Konsulin Tech, an Indonesian IT consulting, software development and business consulting website. Create a premium editorial photograph of two Indonesian software professionals in their early thirties collaboratively reviewing a laptop in a bright modern Denpasar office. One seated man and one woman beside him, natural candid expressions, real work not posed stock handshake. A large desktop monitor softly out of focus with abstract coding shapes, no legible UI. Natural window light, neutral warm white workspace, subtle teal accents, navy clothes. Portrait 2:3 composition with people and laptop in the central two thirds so responsive cover crops work. Realistic skin, materials and proportions. No logos, text, watermarks, no financial charts, no fake branded interfaces. This is illustrative imagery, not identifiable actual staff.

### Software — software.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape software and website development service photo for Konsulin Tech. Create a realistic editorial close-up of an Indonesian developer at a tidy workstation, hands on keyboard with laptop and a second monitor, showing softly focused generic source code blocks and mobile app layout shapes with no readable text. Side angle, main subject desk and devices centered, human face not necessary. Bright neutral modern office, natural daylight, understated teal accessories and navy elements, tactile realism, no futuristic holograms. Landscape 3:2. No brand logos, no watermark, no financial visuals. Do not imply a screenshot of any named product.

### Legal dan bisnis — legal.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape legal and business consulting photo for Konsulin Tech. Premium natural editorial photo of an Indonesian woman business consultant and Indonesian man client at a modern meeting table discussing organized printed business documents and a laptop. Candid professional calm, focused discussion rather than handshake, hands natural and anatomically correct. Unreadable document text, neutral warm white office with a subtle teal upholstered chair and navy folders. Landscape 3:2, keep subjects and documents centrally composed for cover crops. No scales of justice, gavels, law books, financial accounting calculators, logos, watermark or written claims. Illustrative people, not actual named staff.

### Produk dan operasional — products.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape software products and business operations photograph for Konsulin Tech. A realistic clean small Indonesian retail store counter with a tablet cash register, a laptop and smartphone on a neat desk, shelves with boxes softly blurred in background. Screens show generic muted teal and white abstract UI panels without legible text, logos or fabricated real product screenshots. This illustrates SaaS business software, HR administration, invoicing and point-of-sale inventory operations. Premium editorial photography, soft daylight, neutral white and natural wood, navy and teal accents, calm approachable atmosphere. Landscape 3:2, devices centered for responsive cover crop. No accounting or tax consulting theme, no people required, no watermarks, no headlines.
